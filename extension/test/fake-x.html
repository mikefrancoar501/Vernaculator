Town Hall Watch @townhallwatch 2h
Big news from last night's city council meeting. After three hours of debate, the council voted 5 to 2 to replace every streetlight in town with LEDs this spring. The project will cost 2 million dollars, paid from the infrastructure fund. Supporters say lower power bills mean it pays for itself in about six years, and the new lights can dim late at night to cut light pollution. Opponents worried about the up-front cost and asked for a pilot on two streets first. Work starts in March, one neighborhood at a time.
